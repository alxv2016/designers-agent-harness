# doc-reviewer — Complete for its reader

What scores of 0, 2 and 4 look like on this dimension, for the `doc-reviewer` judge. Complete for its reader: what they need to act, nothing they must find elsewhere unannounced. Its weight is in `.agents/evals/weights.json`; how it is scored is the judge's rubric, Quality (.agents/HARNESS.md §12).

**Measured proxies — none.** The judge scores this dimension on its reading alone.

**How to use it.** Find the level the work sits nearest, quote the evidence — a file and line, a screenshot, a measured value — and give 1 or 3 when it sits between two. The person's own examples, under From the person, outrank the ones written here.

## 4 — what good looks like

- Its reader can act from it alone: the rule, its reason, the values, the traps, the open items; each unknown tagged as an open item.

## 2 — acceptable, with a clear gap

- Covers the main case; one step the reader must guess or find in the code.

## 0 — what fails it

- Missing what its reader came for; an unknown stated as known.
