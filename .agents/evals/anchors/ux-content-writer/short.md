# ux-content-writer — Short

What scores of 0, 2 and 4 look like on this dimension, for the `ux-content-writer` judge. As few words as hold the meaning, within the room the screen gives. Its weight is in `.agents/evals/weights.json`; how it is scored is the judge's rubric, Quality (.agents/HARNESS.md §12).

**Measured proxies — built.** `ux_proxies.py` measures strings longer than their element's room (none), and the share with three full stops or "successfully". `verify.py proxies --judge ux-content-writer` runs them; a value out of its range caps this dimension at 2.

**How to use it.** Find the level the work sits nearest, quote the evidence — a file and line, a screenshot, a measured value — and give 1 or 3 when it sits between two. The person's own examples, under From the person, outrank the ones written here.

## 4 — what good looks like

- "Saved to your notes" — every word earns its place; it fits its label without truncation (UX_CONTENT §2.2, §2.4).

## 2 — acceptable, with a clear gap

- Clear but padded: "please", "successfully", a sentence where a phrase would do.

## 0 — what fails it

- A paragraph where a label belongs; truncated in its box.
